01 / Community context

Find a market.
Understand its impact.

Food Access LA is a nonprofit supporting farmers markets across Los Angeles. These markets connect residents with fresh food while supporting farms and small businesses.

Our project brings two needs together: helping residents discover markets, and making the economic impact of market spending easier to understand.

Built for different audiences

Residents need market locations, hours, upcoming events, and information about accepted food assistance benefits.

Staff and supporters need reliable information about how spending at markets flows to farms and small businesses across California.

02 / The platform we’re building

Discovery on the surface.
Reliable data underneath.

01

Market explorer

An interactive map to help residents find nearby markets, discover events, and see which benefits they accept.

02

Impact visualizations

Views that show how market spending and food assistance dollars support farms and small businesses across California.

03

Staff tools

Tools for updating market and event information and reviewing new sales data before it informs public reports.

03 / My contributions

Backend work that makes
the public experience possible.

My contributions focus on APIs for managing market events and tools that validate uploaded sales data. This work helps staff maintain reliable information for the platform’s public maps and impact reports.

Backend developmentEvent APIsData validation

Market event APIs

I’m developing backend APIs for managing market events, supporting the information residents use to discover what is happening at a market.

Sales-data validation

I’m working on validation tools that check uploaded sales data for missing information, duplicate records, and calculation errors.

What the validation work supports

  1. InputUploaded sales data

    Staff bring market sales exports into the platform.

  2. ChecksFind issues early

    Validation identifies missing information, duplicate entries, and inconsistent calculations.

  3. PurposeMore reliable reporting

    Checked data supports the maps and visualizations people use to understand market impact.
